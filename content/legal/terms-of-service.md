## Enquiries and proposals

Sending us an enquiry doesn't create a booking. After we've talked about your day, we'll send a proposal setting out your flowers, styling, delivery and setup, and the price. Prices on our website, including our wedding packages, are starting points. Your proposal confirms the final price for your date, venue and design.

Proposals are valid for the period stated on them. Until you book, your date isn't reserved.

## Booking and payment

Your booking is confirmed once you accept your proposal and pay the deposit it sets out. Because we take a limited number of weddings and events each season, your deposit reserves your date and allows us to begin planning and sourcing for you.

Your proposal and invoices set out the remaining payment amounts and due dates. Please make sure the balance is paid by its due date so we can finalise orders with our growers and suppliers.

## Changes to your order

We're happy to refine your design as your plans come together. Please let us know about changes as early as possible. Some changes may affect the price, and close to your date some changes may not be possible once flowers have been ordered. We'll always tell you before any change affects the cost.

## Seasonal flowers and substitutions

We work with fresh, seasonal flowers, which are natural products affected by weather and growing conditions. Colours, sizes and varieties can vary from photographs, and occasionally a particular flower may be unavailable or below our standard. When that happens we'll substitute flowers of equal or greater value that keep the look, palette and feel of your design.

## Postponing or cancelling

If you need to postpone or cancel, please contact us as soon as possible. If you postpone, we'll do our best to move your booking to your new date, subject to availability.

Your deposit secures your date and our time, and flowers and materials ordered for your event can't be returned, so payments already made may not be refundable. The cancellation terms in your proposal set out exactly what applies. If we ever have to cancel because of circumstances beyond our control, we'll work with you on a fair solution, which may include arranging a trusted florist or refunding the amounts paid for work not completed.

## Delivery, setup and pack-down

Our Classic and Luxe packages include delivery, setup and next-day pack-out within Auckland. For other bookings, delivery, setup and pack-down are included in your proposal. Weddings and events outside Auckland are quoted individually and may include travel costs.

Please make sure we have access to your venue at the times agreed in your proposal, and let us know about any venue rules or restrictions in advance. If access is delayed or restricted for reasons outside our control, we'll do our best to complete your setup, but we can't be responsible for the effect on timing.

## Hire items

Vases, plinths, stands and other items we supply for your day remain our property and are included in your package or proposal. Please take care of them and let us know about any damage. Items that are lost or damaged beyond normal wear may be charged at replacement cost.

## Flower care and safety

Some flowers and foliage can be toxic to pets and people if eaten, and some can stain fabric. Please keep arrangements out of reach of children and pets. Candles and flowers should never be left unattended together.
