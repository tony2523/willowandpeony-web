Willow & Peony is a boutique wedding and event florist on Auckland's North Shore, run by Ivy Diao. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website and when you work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

## The short version

We only collect what we need to reply to you and to design flowers for your wedding or event. We don't sell your information, we don't use advertising or tracking cookies, and we won't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

## What we collect

**When you send an enquiry.** Our wedding, event and general enquiry forms ask for your name, email address and phone number, plus details about your day such as the date, venue, event type, budget, the flowers you're interested in, how you heard about us, and anything you'd like to tell us.

**When you download our Wedding Flower Calendar.** We ask for your name and email address so we can email you the download link, and whether you'd like Ivy to contact you about a free consultation.

**When you book with us.** We keep the details needed to plan and deliver your flowers, such as your proposal, timings, venue and access information, contacts on the day, and payment records.

**When you email or message us.** We keep our correspondence with you.

**When you visit the website.** Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically processes technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

## How we use it

We use your information to:

- reply to your enquiry and prepare a proposal or quote
- email you the Wedding Flower Calendar you requested
- plan, prepare, deliver, set up and pack down your flowers
- invoice you and keep the business and tax records we're required to keep
- keep our website running securely

We only contact you about your enquiry or booking, or about a consultation if you've asked for one.

## Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with trusted providers who help us run the business, and only as much as they need:

- **Cloudflare** hosts our website and protects it from attacks.
- **Resend** delivers the emails our website sends, such as enquiry notifications and calendar emails.
- **Google Workspace** provides our email inboxes.

These providers may store information on servers outside New Zealand, including in the United States. We use providers that protect personal information with safeguards comparable to New Zealand law.

When we work with your venue, planner or other suppliers for your wedding or event, we may share the practical details they need, such as timings and setup information.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

## Photographs of our work

We love sharing the flowers we create. We may photograph our floral designs at your wedding or event for our portfolio, website and Instagram, and we may share images from your photographer with their permission and credit. We'll only feature photos where you or your guests can be identified with your permission. If you'd like a photo removed, email us and we'll take it down.

## Cookies

Our website doesn't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may set a strictly necessary security cookie to protect the site from bots and abuse. If our Instagram feed is shown on the site, the images load from Instagram's servers, which receive your browser's standard technical information.

## How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to respond and follow up. If you book with us, we keep your booking and payment records for at least seven years, as New Zealand tax law requires. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, unless we're legally required to keep it.

## Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we limit access to the people who need it to serve you.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it. Email us at hello@willowandpeony.co.nz and we'll respond as soon as we can, and within 20 working days.

If you have a concern about how we've handled your information, please talk to us first. You can also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner at [privacy.org.nz](https://www.privacy.org.nz).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
