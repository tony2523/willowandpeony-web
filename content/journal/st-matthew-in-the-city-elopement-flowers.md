For Kaelan and Tongtong, their wedding was more than a single day. It was the beginning of a journey through New Zealand—starting with an intimate elopement ceremony at St Matthew-in-the-City in Auckland before continuing to the South Island for travel and more gorgeous photographs.

Beneath the church’s soaring neo-Gothic arches, Kaelan and Tongtong exchanged vows surrounded by elegant white flowers. Cascading orchids adorned the timber pews, delicate petals marked the aisle and refined personal flowers complemented their timeless wedding attire.

![Intimate elopement ceremony beneath the neo-Gothic arches of St Matthew-in-the-City](/images/intimate-elopement-ceremony-beneath-the-neo-gothic-arches-of-st-matthe)

The grandeur of the setting, the simplicity of the floral palette and the couple’s infectious happiness came together in a beautiful celebration of love and adventure.

## A destination elopement in Auckland

Travelling from China to marry in New Zealand gave Kaelan and Tongtong’s elopement a wonderful sense of discovery.

The couple chose St Matthew-in-the-City for their ceremony. Built in 1905, this striking stone church is known for its magnificent neo-Gothic architecture, long central aisle, stained-glass windows and polished timber pews.

![Kaelan and Tongtong sharing a joyful moment outside St Matthew-in-the-City](/images/kaelan-and-tongtong-sharing-a-joyful-moment-outside-st-matthew-in-the)

Although the church has an impressive scale, the ceremony retained the intimacy of an elopement. With the focus entirely on Kaelan, Tongtong and the vows they shared, every moment felt close, calm and meaningful.

![An intimate detail from Kaelan and Tongtong’s wedding ceremony](/images/an-intimate-detail-from-kaelan-and-tongtongs-wedding-ceremony)

![Kaelan and Tongtong with their celebrant at St Matthew-in-the-City](/images/kaelan-and-tongtong-with-their-celebrant-at-st-matthew-in-the-city)

## Cascading white orchids along the aisle

The floral design was created to soften the church while allowing its remarkable architecture to remain central.

Cascading white orchids and roses were arranged at considered intervals along the ends of the pews. Their graceful stems brought natural movement to the aisle and echoed the flowing lines of Tongtong’s veil and lace train.

White petals were scattered lightly along the processional, adding another layer of softness without making the setting feel overly decorated.

Viewed from above, the flowers created an elegant pathway through the church. The contrast between the white orchids, dark timber and pale stone felt luminous, refined and perfectly suited to the couple’s timeless aesthetic.

![White orchid pew flowers lining the aisle at St Matthew-in-the-City](/images/white-orchid-pew-flowers-lining-the-aisle-at-st-matthew-in-the-city)

## Elegant flowers for an intimate ceremony

Tongtong carried a classic white bouquet featuring roses, tulips, lisianthus and stocks. Compact and refined, the bouquet complemented the intricate lacework of her gown while remaining comfortable to carry throughout the ceremony and portrait session. A coordinating white rose buttonhole gave Kaelan’s black suit a polished finishing detail.

![Tongtong’s classic white bridal bouquet by W&P](/images/tongtongs-classic-white-bridal-bouquet-by-w-p)

Personal flowers take on particular importance at an elopement. With fewer decorative elements, the bouquet and buttonhole become part of almost every photograph. They need to look beautiful from every angle while feeling completely connected to the couple and their surroundings.

![White rose wedding buttonhole created for Kaelan](/images/white-rose-wedding-buttonhole-created-for-kaelan)

## Planning a New Zealand elopement

A destination elopement may be intimate, but its details still deserve thoughtful planning.

The ceremony venue, flowers, wedding attire and photography should work together—particularly when the celebration includes travel between several locations. Flowers need to be designed for the scale of the ceremony while also being considered in relation to the photographs that will preserve the experience.

At St Matthew-in-the-City, white roses and orchids work especially beautifully. They bring softness to the neo-Gothic interior, photograph elegantly against the stone and timber, and create a timeless aesthetic that will feel beautiful for years to come.
